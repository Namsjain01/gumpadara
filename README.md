# Gumpadara

**Jautaar · Soreng · West Sikkim · India**

A vision document and website for Gumpadara — a Sherpa hamlet of six households in West Sikkim, and India's first community-owned curated village experience.

---

## About

Gumpadara sits within the larger Gumpadara village in Soreng, West Sikkim. Six Sherpa families. Twelve homestay rooms. A monastery and meditation cave 700 metres into the forest. Terraced farms. A dairy cooperative. A proposed cooperative flower landscape that blooms from spring to late autumn.

This site is a vision document presenting the place, its assets, its experiences, and its future — made to share the idea with the people who need to see it.

---

## What's on the site

- The hamlet and its six named homestays
- The existing assets: monastery trail, wild flora & fauna, farm and dairy, local wines and tongba, forest guide
- Six curated experiences: monastery walk, farming, the Sherpa table, herb walk, dawn birdwatch, tasting the valley
- The flower cooperative — five species across six families, sequenced by season
- The seasonal calendar: Spring, Monsoon, Autumn, Winter, Late Winter
- Who the right guest is
- The community model — six families, one village experience
- Infrastructure planned for the next five years
- Video of the village in motion

---

## Tech

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No framework. No build step.

- **Fonts:** DM Serif Display + DM Sans (Google Fonts)
- **Hosting:** Netlify (with `netlify.toml` cache headers for fast media delivery)
- **Media:** Lazy-loaded images, `preload=metadata` on videos
